To calculate the percentage of the total population in North America that are active social network users, we use the figures provided in the infographic:

- Total population: 351,300,266
- Active social network users: 197,033,600

The calculation is as follows:

(Active social network users / Total population) × 100

= (197,033,600 / 351,300,266) × 100

≈ 56.07%

Therefore, approximately 56.07% of the total population in North America are active social network users.
