Nestlé achieved higher Organic Growth in 2003 than in 2004. Specifically, Organic Growth for 2003 was 5.1%, while for 2004 it was 4.5%. This is confirmed from the chart titled "10 years of the Nestlé Model," which shows the 10-year annual averages and yearly data for Organic Growth, with 2003 at 5.1% and 2004 at 4.5%. The source is the 2011 Full Year Results Roadshow, dated February 21, 2012.
