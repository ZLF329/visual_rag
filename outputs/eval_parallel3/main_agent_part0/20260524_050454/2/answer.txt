In 2011, Organic Growth achieved better performance than Real Internal Growth. Specifically, Organic Growth was 7.5%, while Real Internal Growth was 6.2% — as shown in the bar chart for 2011. This is also supported by the 10-year annual averages, which show Organic Growth at 6.4% versus Real Internal Growth at 3.6%. The data is from the 2011 Full Year Results Roadshow, presented on February 21, 2012.
