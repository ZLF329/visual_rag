As of June 2013, municipal-level government debts were bigger than provincial-level government debts. Specifically, municipal-level debts accounted for 44.5% of the total debts to be repaid by local governments, while provincial-level debts accounted for only 16.3%. This is confirmed in the text from the document, which states: “Municipal level accounts for 44.5%, county and township levels account for 39.2%, while provincial level only accounts for 16.3%.” [Citation: 1shangxichinassubnationaldebts-issuesandsuggestions-160229201053_95/page_05]
