Before irritation of the parietal peritoneum, the following events occur:

1. **Appendiceal obstruction/early appendicitis** — This stage involves visceral peritoneal irritation, which is the initial phase of the disease.
2. **Appendiceal distension** — This follows obstruction and is a key step before the parietal peritoneum becomes irritated.

These stages are depicted in the diagram as occurring sequentially before the irritation of the parietal peritoneum, which is marked by constant RIF (referred ileus) pain and localized peritonitis.
