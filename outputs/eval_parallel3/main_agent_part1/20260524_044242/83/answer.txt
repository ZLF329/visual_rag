To get into content creation, you need to understand the type of content you want to produce, as this determines the vendor, business model, license, and format you will use. This insight is derived from the slide that states: “The content you want determines the vendor, business model, license, and format you will get.” (Page 13, image_ref: 2013alapurchasing-130214144910-phpapp02_95).
