The three types of manufacturing technology for a manufactured assembly of fibres or yarns that has substantial surface area in relation to its thickness and sufficient cohesion to give the assembly useful mechanical strength are **Weaving, Knitting, and Non-Woven**.

This is explicitly stated in the flow chart titled "Flow chart of fabric manufacturing technology" (image_ref: afabric-manufacturing-150314104020-conversion-gate01_95/page_06), which categorizes fabric manufacturing technology into these three main branches. Additionally, the definition of fabric provided on the same page (image_ref: afabric-manufacturing-150314104020-conversion-gate01_95/page_08) confirms that fabric is such an assembly, and weaving is identified as one of the processes for manufacturing it.
