The two approaches for what is also called linkage disequilibrium mapping are:

1. **Family-based Linkage mapping** — This approach involves constructing linkage maps and conducting QTL analysis to identify genomic regions associated with traits (also known as QTL mapping or genome-wide association mapping).

2. **LD-based Association mapping** — This is explicitly labeled as the second main approach in the diagram and is directly referred to as “linkage disequilibrium mapping” in the context of the image.

These two approaches are presented as the “Two main approaches” in the diagram, with “LD-based Association mapping” being the specific term for linkage disequilibrium mapping.
