eBook penetration in School libraries increased from 2011 to 2012. In 2011, it was approximately 45%, and in 2012, it rose to approximately 85%. This is supported by the chart titled "eBook Penetration," which shows the red bar (representing School libraries) rising from around 45% in 2011 to 85% in 2012. The source is the "Library Journal Survey of Ebook Penetration - 2010-2012."
