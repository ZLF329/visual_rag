The level that comes between "all managers between the first-line level and the top level of the organization who manage first-line managers" and Non-managerial Employees is **Middle Managers**.

This is supported by the organizational hierarchy pyramid shown in the image, which clearly lists the levels from top to bottom as:  
1. Top Managers  
2. Middle Managers  
3. First-line Managers  
4. Non-managerial Employees  

Thus, "Middle Managers" are positioned between the first-line managers (who manage first-line managers) and the top level, and directly above the non-managerial employees.  
**Citation**: The pyramid diagram labeled "ORGANIZATIONAL LEVELS (Exhibit 1.1)" from Pearson Education Canada Inc., 2003, page 1.6.
