In a market where not many people are interested in the content (i.e., low market head), but the content still differentiates you effectively, differentiation is **high**.

This corresponds to the **“Niche” quadrant** in the matrix, which is located at the intersection of **Low Market Head** and **High Differentiation**. This quadrant is explicitly labeled as “Niche” and is positioned in the bottom-right corner of the chart, where the x-axis (Differentiation) is high and the y-axis (Market Head) is low.
