A cell membrane disruption drug is one that causes membrane disruption, pore formation, and depolarization, leading to leakage of ions and metabolites. This mechanism involves a general model: (i) attraction of the drug (AP) to the membrane, (ii) attachment of the AP to the membrane, and (iii) insertion of the AP into the membrane, causing its disruption. This process is based on membrane disruption followed by pore formation on the nanometer scale and membrane depolarization.
